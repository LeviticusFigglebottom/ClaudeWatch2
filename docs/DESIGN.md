# RINGFALL — Design Bible

## The world in one breath

2140. Ninety years ago the orbital habitats came down in a slow cascade that never finished. Earth
now sits inside **the Ring**: a permanent band of debris, dead stations and half-alive satellites
that crosses every sky. Nations are gone. What's left are **Charters** — city-states built around
whatever fell nearby — and the **Cores**: intact reactor hearts that still drop from the Ring a few
times a year. A Core powers a Charter for a decade. Whoever holds one holds the future.

Charters don't fight wars. They hire **Runners**: freelance operatives with signature kit, hired in
teams of five to escort, capture or deny Cores at the drop sites. Every match is a drop.

Tone: hopeful, weathered, sunlit. This is not a dystopia; people planted gardens on the wreckage.
Colors are saturated. Architecture is old cities patched with salvage. The Ring is always visible
as a faint arc in the sky, day or night. Humor comes from character, never from parody.

The two teams in any match are **Cinder** (amber/orange, warm) and **Tide** (teal/blue, cool). The
Charters are interchangeable employers; the team colors are contractor colors, not factions.

## Roles

- **Bulwark** (1 per team): frontline. Makes space, absorbs attention, dictates *where* the fight is.
  Role passive: 30% shorter crowd control, 40% less knockback.
- **Striker** (2): damage. Wins duels and converts space into kills. Role passive: +20% move speed
  for 2.5 s after an elimination.
- **Conduit** (2): sustain and enabling. Role passive: out-of-combat regeneration starts after 1.5 s
  instead of 5 s and heals faster.

## Roster — 18 Runners

Every hero has one sentence that no other hero could own. If two heroes share the sentence, one is
wrong. Signature mechanics are marked ★.

### Bulwarks
1. **Ballast** — deep-salvage diver in a pressure suit. ★ **Anchor**: throws a harpoon-anchor that
   either drags him to a surface or reels an enemy to him. Wave-cannon shotgun, Surge (armor while
   walking slow), ult **Riptide**: an expanding tidal pull that drags enemies to a point and slows.
   Heavy, slow, inevitable. Counters flankers; countered by ranged poke.
2. **Cathedral** — Mother Seraphine, a wandering abbess in ceramic plate. ★ **Stained-glass Wall**:
   a barrier that also heals allies standing behind it. Mace primary (wide arc), Censer (short
   dash + AoE damage), ult **Sanctuary**: a dome that blocks damage and cleanses inside.
   Anchor tank; countered by dive-through and long fights.
3. **Kiln** — a furnace-golem industrial worker. Hero resource ★ **Heat** built by taking and
   dealing damage; spends heat on **Vent** (an updraft that launches allies to high ground) and
   **Slag Cast** (temporary wall you can stand on). Primary: molten slugs. Ult **Meltdown**:
   burning ground and massive overhealth. Brawler that builds terrain.
4. **Cairn** — stone-shaper botanist from the Andean Charter. ★ **Raise Slab**: lifts a stone
   pillar under a target point (ally = elevator, enemy = displacement). Primary: rock lobber with
   arc. Bulwark of *height*. Ult **Landslide**: a wave of stone that knocks enemies back and
   leaves cover behind. Countered by fast ranged dps; counters ground-bound melee.
5. **Rook** — gravity engineer in a hover-frame. ★ **Lift**: enemies in a cone float helplessly for
   1.6 s (can still shoot, can't move). Primary: gravity mortar (slow, splash). **Density**: self
   damage reduction + heavier. Ult **Ground Zero**: a gravity well that pulls everything in and
   detonates. Zone tank; countered by unstoppable/cleanse.

### Strikers
6. **Vesper** — lamplighter marksman. ★ **Lantern** reveals enemies through walls to the team.
   Semi-auto rifle, Focus, Zipline, ult **Long Night**.
7. **Harrier** — courier in a jet-rig. ★ **Flight**: hold jump to fly on fuel. Twin SMGs, **Dive**
   (slam down, damage in a circle), **Afterburn** (speed + fuel refill). Ult **Strafing Run**: a
   line of rockets along your flight path. Counters snipers/ground poke; countered by hitscan.
8. **Ricochet** — trick-shot artist with a disc launcher. ★ discs only damage after bouncing at
   least once; each bounce adds damage (up to 3). **Bank Shot**: next disc homes after its first
   bounce. **Skip**: short dash that leaves a bouncing disc behind. Ult **Pinball**: fills the area
   with bouncing discs for 6 s. Rewards geometry knowledge; countered by open ground.
9. **Coil** — arc-welder turned duelist. ★ **Chain**: primary lightning that jumps between enemies
   within 6 m (up to 3, decaying). **Tesla Node**: a deployable that zaps nearby enemies and can be
   chained *through*. **Capacitor**: absorbs incoming damage for 1.5 s, releases it as a burst.
   Ult **Blackout**: silences and slows all enemies in 14 m for 3 s. Counters grouped teams.
10. **Wisp** — a cartographer who folds space. ★ **Exchange**: swap positions with a placed marker
    or an enemy you hit with the marked needle. Burst needle rifle. **Mark** (place/teleport).
    Ult **Displacement**: swap every enemy in 10 m with the positions of your marks. Ultimate
    disruptor; countered by unstoppable heroes and quick burst.
11. **Bombard** — artillery spotter. ★ indirect fire: the mortar's landing point shows through walls
    as a reticle; shells arc over cover. **Spotter drone**: reveals a zone. **Airburst**: next shell
    explodes above targets (no cover). Ult **Barrage**: 12 shells on a marked 10 m area. Counters
    stationary bulwarks; countered by dive.
12. **Sable** — shadow-dancer, melee only. ★ **Backstab**: hits from behind deal 2.5×. **Shroud**:
    invisible while below 40% movement speed. **Requiem** (ult): marks all enemies in 12 m, dashes
    through them one by one. Weapon: twin blades (three-hit combo). Counters supports and snipers;
    countered by reveal and AoE.
13. **Bramble** — ex-terraformer. ★ **Roots**: her thorn shots root on the third consecutive hit on
    the same target. **Snare** (root vine), **Thicket** (a thorn wall that damages when crossed).
    Ult **Overgrowth**: roots everyone in 12 m for 2.5 s and heals allies inside. Counters mobile
    heroes; countered by cleanse and range.

### Conduits
14. **Tallow** — a candle-maker who sells light. ★ **Wicks**: places up to three candles that heal
    allies within 6 m; enemies who snuff them (walk into them) get burned. Primary: flame bolts.
    **Snuff**: cleanse + 1 s of invulnerability on an ally. Ult **Vigil**: allies in 12 m cannot drop
    below 1 hp for 5 s. Positional healer; countered by dive onto candles.
15. **Cadence** — a street musician with a bass-cannon. ★ **On the beat**: her aura heals on every
    beat (120 bpm); healing is doubled when she fires on-beat. **Crescendo**: speed boost pulse.
    **Discord**: enemies in a cone take +25% damage. Ult **Anthem**: 300 overhealth to allies in
    14 m. Tempo support; countered by silence and by splitting the team.
16. **Lumen** — optics researcher with a mirror-staff. ★ beam that **bounces off placed mirrors**
    (heals allies / damages enemies along the path). **Prism**: the beam splits to hit three
    targets. **Refract**: place a mirror. Ult **Sunstroke**: a wide beam that blinds and burns.
    Geometry healer; countered by mobility that breaks lines.
17. **Suture** — a field medic with a stapler-gun. ★ **Tether**: links two allies for 6 s; healing
    on one is copied to the other. **Bandage volley**: healing shells that also do small damage.
    **Adrenaline**: an ally gets +25% fire rate and move speed. Ult **Triage**: everyone within 10 m
    is healed to full and cleansed. Burst healer; countered by tether-breaking displacement.
18. **Ferry** — a boat-woman of the drowned cities; carries the dead. ★ **Waystone**: a beacon
    allies can teleport to from spawn. **Lantern bolts** (heal/damage). **Undertow**: pulls an ally
    to you. Ult **Crossing**: resurrects up to two dead allies near her over 3 s. Tempo support;
    countered by targeting the beacon and killing her first.

### Counter-web (summary)
Reveal (Vesper, Bombard) counters stealth (Sable) and mobility. Cleanse (Tallow, Suture, Cathedral
ult) counters roots/DoTs (Bramble, Kiln, Coil). Unstoppable (Kiln during Meltdown, Rook during
Density) counters displacement (Wisp, Ballast, Rook, Cairn). Barriers (Cathedral, Kiln, Cairn)
counter hitscan poke (Vesper, Coil) but not arcs (Bombard, Ricochet). Dive (Harrier, Sable, Wisp)
counters artillery and snipers; hitscan (Vesper, Coil) counters dive.

### Synergies
Vesper→Bombard (reveal + artillery), Rook Lift→Bombard/Coil, Ballast Riptide→Coil Blackout→Rook
Ground Zero (the "drain"), Cathedral Wall + Vesper, Cairn Slab + Vesper/Bombard, Cadence + Harrier,
Ferry Waystone + any dive comp, Suture Tether + a bulwark, Tallow Vigil + Sable Requiem.

Nightmarket: Coil; Orchard: Bramble; Meridian: Rook) and environmental storytelling that points at
them without a word of dialogue.

## Combat feel commitments

- Hitscan hits register the frame you click. Tracers spawn from the muzzle you see.
- Every damage instance produces: a hitmarker, a number, a target flash, and a sound whose pitch
  tells you what you hit (body / head / barrier).
- Kills: 50 ms hitstop, a distinct chime, and a name in the feed within the same frame.
- Recoil is a *pattern*, not noise; spread is visible on the crosshair.
- Getting hit: directional indicator, flinch away from the source, red vignette below 35%.
- Ultimates have a call-out stinger everyone hears, friendly and enemy variants.
