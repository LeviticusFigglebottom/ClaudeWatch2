- **Kenney** (https://kenney.nl) — 3D kits under `assets/models/<kit>/` (file names keep Kenney's

  material listed per file in `assets/audio/ATTRIBUTION.md`. "Kenney" assets are CC0; support them

- **ambientCG** (https://ambientcg.com, by Lennart Demes) — PBR texture sets under
  `assets/textures/<material>/` (albedo/normal/roughness/ao). The exact ambientCG asset id per
  material is recorded in `assets/textures/SOURCES.txt`. Materials marked "procedurally generated"
  there were made for this project.
- **Poly Haven** (https://polyhaven.com) — HDRI panoramas under `assets/hdri/`, 2K `.hdr`.
